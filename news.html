BSCB / GenSoc 2027 Conference

The BSCB / GenSoc 2027 Conference, a joint meeting of the British Society for Cell Biology and The Genetics Society, will take place 19-22 April 2027 at Queen Mary University of London

The meeting will bring together an exceptional group of speakers and researchers to explore how genetic, epigenetic, and molecular mechanisms shape dynamic cellular and subcellular processes across diverse model systems. Most importantly, it will be a fantastic opportunity to connect with colleagues across traditionally separate disciplines and foster new collaborations.

Abstract submissions are now open, and we would be delighted to welcome researchers at all career stages. There are also a range of opportunities available to support participation in the meeting.

Please consider submitting an abstract and joining us in London. I'd also be very grateful if you could share this post with colleagues, students, collaborators, and anyone who may be interested, helping us reach the widest possible audience.

We look forward to welcoming you for what promises to be an engaging and inspiring meeting!
